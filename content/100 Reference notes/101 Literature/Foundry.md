> [!tldr] Abstract
> Foundation models pre-trained with self-supervised learning (SSL) on large-scale datasets have become powerful general-purpose feature extractors. However, their immense size and computational cost make them prohibitive for deployment on edge devices such as robots and AR/VR headsets. Existing compression techniques like standard knowledge distillation create efficient ‘specialist’ models but sacrifice the crucial, downstream-agnostic generality that makes foundation models so valuable.
